# MacPaint User's Guide: outline

Stage 1, 21 October 2026. The book's chapters and sections, in the
family shape (D6): Learning, Using, Reference, then appendixes,
glossary and index. Each section names its figures (codes from
`figures.md`) and its sources:
- **[S]** the 1.3 source;
- **[B]** the 1.5/1.6 binary;
- **[T]** a test in Mini vMac or on the Mac Plus;
- **[MP]** the original Apple manual, for approach only.

Anything not yet confirmed is listed in `fact-check.md`. Page counts
are estimates at A5. **The total is about 120 pages.**

## Chapter 2: Using MacPaint (about 42 pages)

Opener spread, the chapter's contents page, then task pages (style
guide §2). Each group starts with a short introduction.

**Painting and Drawing**
- To paint with the paintbrush; to choose a brush shape (double-click
  the paintbrush); to paint with Brush Mirrors.
- To use the spraypaint.
- To draw with the pencil. A pencil line starts white if you begin on a
  black dot.
- To draw straight lines: Shift gives 45° and 90°; Option draws in the
  pattern.
- To draw shapes: hollow or filled, with or without a border, and with
  a patterned border.
- To draw freehand shapes; to draw polygons, including how to finish
  one.
- To fill an area with the paint can, and what to do when paint leaks.
- To paint without covering what's underneath (⌘ held) **[CHECK]**.

**Patterns**
- To choose a pattern.
- To edit a pattern (double-click it, or Goodies › Edit Pattern).
- To pick up a pattern from your picture.
- Patterns are saved with each document.

**Erasing:** four ways:
- the eraser;
- double-clicking the eraser;
- selecting, then pressing Backspace or choosing Clear;
- Undo.

**Selecting and Changing**
- The lasso and the selection rectangle compared: the lasso shrinks to
  the shapes, while the rectangle keeps the background.
- To select the whole window.
- To move, to move in a straight line (Shift), and to copy (Option).
- To make many copies (Option-⌘), with the line width setting the
  spacing.
- To stretch or shrink (⌘); to keep the proportions (Shift-⌘).
- Invert, Fill and Trace Edges (Shift for a shadow **[CHECK]**).
- Flip Horizontal and Flip Vertical; Rotate. These four need the
  selection rectangle, not the lasso.

**Cut, Copy and Paste**
- The Clipboard: what it holds and how long.
- To copy and paste within a picture.
- To paste into a selected area (the picture is scaled to fit).
- Pasting a lasso selection (only the shapes come back).
- To paste text from another program; text you can still change, and
  ⌘-drag to rewrap it.
- To move pictures between documents (Close, then Open).
- To move pictures between programs.
- **The Scrapbook:** to keep pictures; to paste from it.
- **The Clipboard and the Scrapbook compared.**
- **When MacPaint can't copy:** the alert, and what you see in 1.6
  (Appendix A) **[CHECK]**.

**Text**
- To type in a picture.
- To change font, size and style while typing; ⌘-< and ⌘-> for size,
  ⌘-Shift-< and ⌘-Shift-> for font **[CHECK]**.
- Alignment.
- To start a new block of text with Enter **[CHECK]**.
- Limits: 20 lines of 80 characters.
- Why the Font, FontSize and Style menus are dimmed while a picture is
  selected.

**Detailed Work**
- FatBits: entering it by double-clicking the pencil or ⌘-clicking;
  moving around in it with Option and the pencil; leaving it.
- The Grid.

**The Whole Page**
- Show Page: moving the picture, and the warning that dragging it off
  the page can't be undone (an **Important** note).
- The grabber; Shift to move in a straight line.

**Documents**
- New and Open. Only one document can be open at a time (an
  **Important** note).
- Save and Save As; replacing a document.
- Revert (an **Important** note: it can't be undone).
- Close and Quit, and their Command keys.
- Opening a MacPaint document from the Finder.
- The "rescue" window after a crash.

**Printing**
- Print Draft and Print Final.
- Print Catalog.
- Printing from the Finder.
- There is no Page Setup.

## Appendixes (about 14 pages)

- **A, Messages.** Every alert and dialog text, exact:
  - the 15 alerts (ALRT 2101–2115) and the Close/Quit save dialogs;
  - what each means and what to do.
- **B, MacPaint and Your Disks:**
  - the work files and the free space they need, checked all the time;
  - the "rescue" window;
  - where the Clipboard lives (the startup disk's System Folder);
  - keeping MacPaint on any disk (1.6);
  - MultiFinder.
- **C, Keyboard Shortcuts:**
  - menu keys;
  - the \` key (Undo) and Backspace (Clear);
  - modifier keys with each tool;
  - double-clicks.
- **D, Troubleshooting.** Covers:
  - the disk-full and "almost full" alerts;
  - "trouble writing the scrap";
  - a pasted picture that is scaled;
  - paint leaking;
  - Rotate and Flip dimmed;
  - the Font menu dimmed;
  - text that stops at 80 characters;
  - the Catalog missing pictures in folders.

  Normal behaviour and real failures are kept apart.
- **E, About MacPaint 1.6:**
  - what's different from 1.5: the Clipboard from any disk, ⌘-N/W/Q,
    the ⌘-Quit restart removed, a failed Copy keeps your picture;
  - the version shown in About MacPaint.
